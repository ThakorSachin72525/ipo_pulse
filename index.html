Time-stamped grey market premium observations help show how market expectations change.

04

Actual prices and accuracy

After listing data becomes available, actual listing prices and historical prediction accuracy can be reviewed.

How it works

Observe, estimate, compare.

  1. 1

    IPO information and current GMP observations are collected from the configured data source.

  2. 2

    IPO Pulse records estimates based on the available issue price and GMP observation.

  3. 3

    When an IPO lists and actual data is available, the estimate can be compared with the outcome.

GMP is unofficial market information.

Grey market premium figures may be delayed or inaccurate. They are not guaranteed listing prices and should not be treated as investment advice.
